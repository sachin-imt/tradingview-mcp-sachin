Macro Thesis — Aug–Sep 2026

AJ's PEG Definition — Growth One Year Out, Not Next Year

Sep 3 · X

AJ spelled out the methodology behind the Cockpit's y-axis for the first time. His growth term is NTM+1 earnings ÷ NTM earnings — not this year's growth, but the growth the market expects over the twelve months starting a year from now.

The reasoning: "Investors should not focus on the next twelve months. Investors should focus on what investors believe the next twelve months in twelve months can deliver." Forward P/E already captures NTM; pairing it with NTM growth would double-count the same window. Rolling NTM intervals are used throughout rather than calendar years, since what matters is a consistent 1-year period, not January-to-December.

This matters for reading the Cockpit: a low PEG on AJ's definition means the market is cheaply pricing growth that is still two years out — which is exactly the AVGO argument below.

August CPI (Sep 11) — Oil Base Effect Sets Up a Favorable Print

Sep 2 · X

The oil scare is misread. "Oil is freaking investors out" — but the accurate framing is that oil is worrying investors that other investors would be selling. August 2026 delivered oil at $83, comparable to June's $82. In June, oil contributed 86bps (0.86pp) to headline CPI.

The base effect is what matters. June 2025 average oil was $67; August 2025 was $64 — $3 lower. So August 2026 compares against a lower 2025 bar than the June 2026 print did. The August 2026 oil contribution to CPI will therefore likely exceed June's 86bps.

Why that is bullish, not bearish. July CPI printed 3.4%. Consensus (incomplete) points to another 3.4% for August. If headline holds flat while the energy contribution rises, the arithmetic implies non-energy inflation drivers actually eased — a genuinely positive signal that the market is currently mispricing as an oil problem.

AJ's read: "I don't want to sound too bullish but there is a reasonably likely pathway for a favorable development next week." August CPI drops Friday, September 11. Separately, AJ reposted his May thesis: the administration stays focused on pulling pump prices down ahead of the Midterms.

US Credit Market Update — Constructive at Index, Cautionary in Tail

Aug 30 · Portal

Credit signal is broadly reassuring for US equities. IG spreads unchanged at 79bps, BBB at 98bps, broad HY tightened 14bps to 263bps — near bottom decile since financial crisis. All-in cost of debt fell for both cohorts (IG 5.43→5.38%, HY 7.19→6.96%). August was easing, not tightening.

Lone deterioration: CCC. Widened 40bps to 1,031bps — through the 1,000 distress threshold, fourth consecutive widening at ~24bps/mo. Single B minus BB actually compressed 5bps, meaning stress has not migrated up the quality curve. Contained default cycle in weakest decile, not a systemic tell.

Escalation trigger to watch: BB spreads through 200bps (currently 153). That is the single level that flips the equity call. Everything else on the page is secondary.

Actionable splits: Screen leveraged equity holdings by rating & maturity wall, not by leverage ratio. Danger zone: net debt > 5x, rated CCC/B-, maturities in 2027–28. Best positioned: profitable mega-cap with strong balance sheets. Small caps face headwind from CCC minus BB quality spread widening 53bps.
