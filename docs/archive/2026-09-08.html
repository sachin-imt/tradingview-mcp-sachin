Details — Corridor Bands & Forecast Confidence

The definitive corridor table. Bands are the implied share price at each standard deviation of the observable forward multiple over the lookback window, evaluated at today's NTM earnings — which is why these medians sit far below the Overview's implied upside, where the corridor is projected forward a year along its earnings slope.
